Edgar Von Haupt's Letter

The most natural front door into FRH is still a personal invitation: one earnest man, one hidden chapter house, and the suspicion that the world is larger than it appears.

This is the campaign's diegetic invitation. It is useful not only because it sets the mystery in motion, but because it reveals the emotional posture of the game: curiosity, secrecy, trust, scholarship, danger, and the sense that old things may still be waiting for the right people to notice them.

Edgar Von Haupt
1666 Fila de Horca Blvd.
Cloudburg, Colorado

April 20th, 1923

Dear Friend,

I know this letter comes as a surprise. Let me assure you that I am writing to you as one of a select few. I have a proposal for you that I believe will interest you very much.

I am a trance medium, and it was through occult means that I was led to contact you. I am confident you will keep what follows in confidence even if you choose not to accept my invitation.

In January of 1921, soon after purchasing my house in Cloudburg as both a home and an office, I had a vivid dream. In it I sat at a table with a number of mediaeval knights, all of them bearing an unmistakable air of purpose and assistance. Before me was a list of names and addresses written in my own hand. When I awoke, I found that I had written the very same list at my desk. There was also a map.

Your name and address were on that list.

The map proved stranger still. It was a map of my own basement, but it showed sub-levels beneath the one I knew. After weeks of clearing space, I uncovered a bricked-over doorway. Beyond it lay a hidden chamber full of dust, manuscripts, relics, and objects whose purpose I could not safely guess.

The artifacts were beyond my own powers of interpretation, but I was convinced that what I had found was not merely historical. It felt protected, dangerous, and old in a way that resists ordinary language. Through the same occult sensibility that led me to you, I found one trustworthy scholar: Dr. David Blunth of Isla Oro University.

Together we began translating one of the recovered scrolls. It referred to an ancient organization called the Followers of the Right Hand. According to the text, my house had once served as one of their chapter houses or safe houses before being closed for decades. The same materials speak of monsters, possessing spirits, ghosts, and other supernatural threats that were not treated as superstition but as active dangers requiring disciplined response.

Dr. Blunth and I have concluded that these discoveries are too important to be ignored and too dangerous to be handled carelessly. We intend to take up the mission of the FRH in what limited way we can. We hope you will consider joining us.

From my study in Cloudburg I maintain correspondence with scholars, explorers, occult investigators, and other unusual people across the world. Strange rumors, troubling letters, and unsettling visions reach me with increasing frequency. When something demands investigation, I would like to know that I can call upon trusted agents to learn the truth.

If nothing else, I ask that you come to Cloudburg, see the hidden rooms for yourself, and speak with us before deciding. If you decline, please keep this letter safe and destroy it once you have made up your mind.

I look forward to your reply.

Warmly,
Edgar Von Haupt
Psychic Medium
