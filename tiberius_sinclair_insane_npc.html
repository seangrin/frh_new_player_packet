Description

Scion of the immensely wealthy Sinclair Family, with Sinclair Electricity Lumber and Chemicals owning most of Idaho and big chunks of Wyoming and British Columbia. By day he is the rakishly handsome playboy and socialite. By night he becomes The Wraith, a psychically gifted occult operative. That man is gone now in any practical sense.

This sheet preserves what remains after catastrophic knowledge exposure and psychological collapse. The attributes and advantages are still written on paper, but they no longer add up to a functioning investigator's life. Tiberius survives as a case study in what FRH means when it says forbidden knowledge can cost more than sanity in the abstract. It can cost coherence, mobility, usefulness, trust, and the ability to continue as oneself.
