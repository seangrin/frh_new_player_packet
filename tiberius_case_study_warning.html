Tiberius Sinclair - Catastrophic Knowledge Exposure

During an investigation for the Followers of the Right Hand, Tiberius Sinclair uncovered a partial folio of De Vermis Mysteriis. This was part of the FRH's occult research into irregularities in maritime cargo and occult financial transactions.

Despite FRH protocol, which mandates psychic containment, buffering, and supervised readings of such dangerous materials, Tiberius chose to read the text privately and unprepared.

What followed was not a summoning, a gate-opening, or a manifested entity, but a waking intrusion: yellow curtains, electric light hum, smell of ink, a bookmark resting in the page. In less than a minute, his perception fused with a metaphysical presence, leaving him psychologically shattered.

Mechanical Outcome

Tiberius experienced a catastrophic cascade of Fright and Derangement, leading to a loss of his faculties and abilities far beyond normal limits. The resulting state includes phobic fragmentation, addiction, Combat Paralysis, Total Pacifism, Social Withdrawal, delusional severance from his psionic abilities, and compulsive destruction of written material.

His effective character value was reduced from 375 points to 25 points. His attributes and advantages remain on paper, but they are functionally unusable as a playable investigator's life.

Why This Matters in the Packet

Tiberius is permanently retired from play. He is alive, institutionalized, and no longer available as an active investigator. That is why he matters as a case study. He demonstrates that forbidden knowledge in FRH is not just a mood word. It can destroy a life without requiring a monster to physically tear anyone apart.

He is not merely a cautionary tale about recklessness. He is a demonstration of the fundamental danger of knowing too much, too quickly, without the structures that might have kept the mind intact.

If you want the full retired-state mechanical picture, open Tiberius Sinclair - Insane NPC Example.
